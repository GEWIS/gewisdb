revertRelated()

SubDecision.php : 97

Reverts the related entities of a subdecision in ReportDB, i.e. undoes {@see self::generateRelated()}.

public revertRelated(SubDecision $subDecision) : void

The subdecision itself is left alone: it remains part of the historical record, it is its decision that is marked as annulled. Only the entities that were derived from it are removed or reset.

Whether reverting is allowed at all is decided in the Database module, which owns the ledger; by the time a subdecision gets here it may simply be reverted. Reverting is however tolerant of missing related entities: during a full (re)generation the decisions are processed before the organ, board, and keyholder tables are built, so there may be nothing to revert yet.
